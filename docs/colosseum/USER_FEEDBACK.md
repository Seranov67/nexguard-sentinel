# Operator validation register

Status: first human session started, 1 October 2026. The owner chose themselves
as operator one. No independent operator, customer, revenue or demand is claimed.

## Hypothesis and predeclared checks

Target user hypothesis: a small Base protocol team member who must inspect a
withdrawal alert, explain the evidence and leave a handover record.
The proposed benefit is less manual assembly of source facts and review history.
An owner session can reveal usability problems; it cannot establish market demand.

Target for the first complete walkthrough: at most eight minutes to inspect one
case, record a disposition and export its brief, with at most one explanation
from the facilitator. This is a target, not a measured performance claim.

At the end, ask the operator to explain:

1. Why the rule opened this case and which units are being compared.
2. Which receipt/source facts were verified and which action evidence is absent.
3. What acknowledge/resolve record and whether a contract action occurred.
4. Whether the exported brief is sufficient for a colleague to continue the review.

Record elapsed time, facilitator interventions, errors, missing information and
the operator's own wording. Obtain any later external participant's actual feedback.
